About Universiti Malaysia Terengganu

Universiti Malaysia Terengganu (UMT) is a premier public university in Malaysia that specializes in marine science, aquatic resources, and oceanography. Originally established as a fisheries and marine science center under Universiti Pertanian Malaysia (UPM), it has grown into an independent, full-fledged institution dedicated to sustainable marine development and environmental conservation.

Located in Kuala Nerus, Terengganu, UMT provides state-of-the-art research facilities, a dedicated campus marine hatchery, and a fleet of research vessels. The university continually strives to generate cutting-edge knowledge through academic excellence, innovation, and community partnerships.
